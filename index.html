Prologue

Shuru karne se pehle

ek chhoti si baat

Yeh kahani kisi film jaisi nahi hai. Na hero hai, na villain. Bas ek aam banda — jo apni zindagi jee raha hai, jaise tum, jaise main.

Kabhi lagta hai sab bekaar hai. Kabhi lagta hai sab theek ho jayega. Dono sach hain. Dono saath chalte hain.

— chalo, shuru karte hain —

Chapter 01

Shuruaat

jahaan se sab shuru hua

Gali chhoti thi — sirf 8 ghar the. Mod pe ek peepal ka ped tha, jiske neeche humne 3 cricket finals khoye the. Dopahar mein dhoop itni tez thi ki chhat ka cement pairon ko jalata tha.

Par sham 5 baje ke baad, jab maa ki rasoi se chai ki khushbu aati thi — woh mera favourite time tha.

Aur main bhi badla.

Sach kahun toh bachpan mein kuch khaas nahi tha. Ek aam sa bachcha — aam se sapne, aam si zindagi. Par jab peeche mudke dekhta hoon, wahi aam cheezein sabse khoobsurat lagti hain.

Maa kehti thi — "beta, jo bhi ban, achha ban." Tab samajh nahi aaya tha. Ab samajh aata hai.

"Bada hoke kya banega?"
— koi poochta tha.
"Pata nahi," main kehta tha. "Par kuch aisa, jo yaad rahe."

Dopahar ki dhoop, aam ke ped, aur chhat pe leti hui sham — yeh sab aaj bhi aankhon ke saamne aa jaate hain. Jaise kal ki baat ho.

· · ·

Bachpan mein ek baat samajh nahi aati thi — ki waqt itni tezi se guzarta hai. Lagta tha ye din kabhi khatam nahi honge. Par khatam ho gaye.

Kuch cheezein wapas nahi aati.

Bas yaadein reh jaati hain. Aur yahi kahani unhi yaadon ki hai.

"Har yaad ek kahani hai,
aur har kahani ek zindagi."

Chapter 02

Safar

jo raaste mile, jo log mile

17 saal ki umar mein pehli baar train chadhi. Window seat mili. Bahar khet the, andar ghabrahat. Ghar se 800 km door ja raha tha — pehli baar akela.

"Tu akela hai? Ghar nahi yaad aata?"
— kisi ne poocha tha.
"Aata hai," maine kaha. "Par ruk nahi sakta."

Shehar ne bahut kuch sikhaya. Kaise ladna hai, kaise gir ke uthna hai, kaise apne aap pe bharosa karna hai. Kuch log mile jo aaj bhi saath hain. Kuch chale gaye — unki yaadein reh gayi.

Har mod pe kuch naya mila.

· · ·

Din bhaari the, raatein lambi. Kabhi laga sab bekaar hai. Par subah phir se uthna — wahi asli himmat hai.

Ek raat yaad hai — chhat pe akela baitha tha, neeche shehar ki roshni. Lagta tha sab itna bada hai, aur main itna chhota. Par usi raat samajh aaya — chhota hone mein bhi taakat hai. Kyunki chhota insaan hi sabse zyada seekh sakta hai.

— aur main seekhta raha —

Chapter 03

Tootna

jab sab kuch bikhar gaya

Har kahani mein ek aisa mod aata hai, jahan sab tootne lagta hai. Mera bhi aaya.

Kuch khaas nahi hua tha — bas zindagi ne apna kaam kiya. Log chale gaye, sapne toote, bharosa hil gaya. Aur main akela reh gaya — apne andheron ke saath.

Wahi sabse mushkil waqt tha.

Raat ke 3 baje tak chhat pe baitha tha. Phone silent tha. 47 messages dekhe the maine — koi reply nahi aaya. Subah hote hi lagta tha — aaj phir wahi din.

"Tum theek ho?"
— kisi ne poocha.
"Haan," maine kaha.
Jhooth bola tha.

Par ek din — pata nahi kaise — main utha. Nahaya. Kapde pehne. Bahar nikla. Tapri pe chai 10 rupaye ki thi. Haath kaanp rahe the. Par pehla ghoont liya — aur pehli baar mehsoos hua ki zinda hoon.

· · ·

Tootna zaroori hai. Kyunki jab sab kuch toot jaata hai, tab pata chalta hai — asli cheez kya thi. Jo bacha, wahi sach tha.

Chapter 04

Confusion

jab kuch samajh nahi aa raha tha

Tootne ke baad ek aur cheez aayi — confusion. Sab bikhar gaya tha, par sabse bada sawaal yeh tha — ab kya?

Raat bhar jaagta tha, sochta tha. Kya karoon? Kahan jaoon? Kaun sa rasta sahi hai? Koi jawab nahi milta tha. Bas sawaal hi sawaal.

Sabse mushkil sawaal — "main kaun hoon?"

Log kehte the — "tu strong hai, tu kar lega." Par andar se main toota hua tha. Bahar muskura deta tha, andar khaali tha.

"Tu theek hai?"
— kisi ne poocha.
"Haan," maine kaha.
Par is baar khud ko bhi yakeen nahi tha.

· · ·

Ek din chhat pe baitha tha, chai haath mein. Neeche shehar ki roshni. Tab samajh aaya — confusion bura nahi hota. Confusion matlab tu abhi bhi soch raha hai. Tu abhi bhi zinda hai.

Jo log kabhi confused nahi hote, woh shayad kabhi kuch naya try hi nahi karte. Confusion ek signal hai — ki tum badal rahe ho.

Aur badalna zaroori hai.

— sawaal hi jawab tak le jaate hain —

"Jo girta hai, wahi
uthna seekhta hai."

Chapter 05

Uthna

phir se khade hone ki kahani

Uthna aasan nahi tha. Par ek baar uth gaya, toh rukna nahi tha.

Chhoti chhoti cheezein — ek acchi chai, ek purana gaana, ek dost ka message — inhi ne sahara diya. Bade sapne nahi, chhote chhote lamhe.

Aur wahi lamhe zindagi ban gaye.

Maine likhna shuru kiya. Pehle bas apne liye. Phir kisi ne padha, aur kaha — "yeh toh meri bhi kahani hai". Tab samajh aaya, kahaniyan sirf apni nahi hoti. Sabki hoti hain.

"Tum likhte ho?"
— kisi ne poocha.
"Haan," maine kaha. "Ab toh likhta hoon."

Chapter 06

Aaj

jahaan main ab hoon

Aaj main yahan hoon. Wahi bachcha, par thoda samajhdaar. Wahi sapne, par thoda sach ke qareeb.

Ab bhi galtiyan karta hoon. Ab bhi seekh raha hoon. Farq sirf itna hai — ab apne aap pe sharm nahi aati.

Yeh kahani abhi khatam nahi hui.

Bas ek page palta hai. Baaki tum padhte raho — main likhta rahunga.

— shukriya, padhne ke liye —

Kuch Khaas

Na tamanna hai sitaron ki,
na chahat hai nazaron ki.

Na mujhe sitaron ki tamanna hai, na nazaron ki chahat hai.

Jaan, tum jaise dost mile,
toh kya zarurat hai hazaron ki.

Agar tum jaise dost mil gaye, toh hazaron ki kya zarurat?

· · ·

Bohot afzal ho tum —
tumhe nayaab likh doon,
yeh nazmein, ghazlein chhodo,
main tum pe kitaab likh doon.

Tum bohot afzal ho — tumhe nayaab likh doon. Yeh nazmein aur ghazlein chhod do, main toh tum pe ek poori kitaab likh doon.

· · ·

Tum chand ki baat karte ho,
hum chand se baat karte hain ✨🌙

Tum chand ki tareef karte ho — hum toh chand se hi baat karte hain.

Koi kitna bhi khoobsurat ho —
tera jaisa toh nahi ho sakta 🥀❤️

Koi bhi kitna bhi khoobsurat ho, tumhare jaisa toh koi nahi ho sakta.

Na sitaron ki tamanna rahi,
na nazaron ki chahat rahi.
Jab tum mile — toh sab kuch mil gaya.

Ab na sitaron ki tamanna rahi, na nazaron ki chahat. Jab tum mile, toh sab kuch mil gaya.
