Prologue

Shuru karne se pehle

ek chhoti si baat

Yeh kahani kisi kitaab ki tarah nahi hai. Na iska koi hero hai, na koi villain. Bas ek aam sa insaan — jo apni zindagi jee raha hai, jaise tum, jaise main.

Kabhi lagta hai sab kuch bekaar hai. Kabhi lagta hai sab kuch theek ho jayega. Dono sach hain. Dono ek saath chalte hain.

— chalo, shuru karte hain —

Chapter 01

Shuruaat

jahaan se sab kuch shuru hua

Yahan apni kahani ka pehla hissa likho. Jaise — main kahan paida hua, bachpan kaisa tha, ghar kaisa tha, maa-baap kaise the.

Yaad hai mujhe, wo chhoti si gali, wo peepal ka ped, aur wo sham ki chai ki khushbu. Bachpan mein lagta tha ye sab kabhi nahi badlega. Par badla — sab kuch badla.

Aur main bhi badla.

Sach kahun toh shuruaat mein kuch khaas nahi tha. Ek aam sa bachcha, aam se sapne, aam si zindagi. Par jab peeche mudke dekhta hoon, toh wahi aam cheezein sabse khoobsurat lagti hain.

Maa kehti thi — "beta, jo bhi ban, achha ban." Tab samajh nahi aaya tha. Ab samajh aata hai.

"Bada hoke kya banega?"
— koi poochta tha.
"Pata nahi," main kehta tha. "Par kuch aisa, jo yaad rahe."

Dopahar ki dhoop, aam ke ped, aur chhat pe leti hui sham — yeh sab yaadein aaj bhi aankhon ke saamne aa jaati hain. Jaise kal ki baat ho.

· · ·

Bachpan mein ek baat samajh nahi aati thi — ki waqt itni tezi se guzarta hai. Lagta tha ye din kabhi khatam nahi honge. Par khatam ho gaye. Aur ab wahi din yaad aate hain, toh dil bhar aata hai.

Kuch cheezein wapas nahi aati.

Bas yaadein reh jaati hain. Aur yahi kahani unhi yaadon ki hai.

"Har yaad ek kahani hai,
aur har kahani ek zindagi."

Chapter 02

Safar

jo raaste mile, jo log mile

Gaon se shehar ka safar — wo pehli train, wo pehla akela din, wo pehli baar khud ka kamra. Sab kuch naya tha, sab kuch ajnabi.

"Tu akela hai? Ghar nahi yaad aata?"
— kisi ne poocha tha.
"Aata hai," maine kaha. "Par ruk nahi sakta."

Shehar ne bahut kuch sikhaya. Kaise ladna hai, kaise gir ke utna hai, kaise apne aap pe bharosa karna hai. Kuch log mile jo aaj bhi saath hain. Kuch log chale gaye — unki yaadein reh gayi.

Har mod pe kuch naya mila.

· · ·

Din bhaari the, raatein lambi. Kabhi laga sab bekaar hai. Par subah phir se uthna — wahi asli himmat hai.

Ek raat yaad hai — chhat pe akela baitha tha, neeche shehar ki roshni. Lagta tha sab kuch itna bada hai, aur main itna chhota. Par usi raat ek baat samajh aayi — chhota hone mein bhi ek taakat hai. Kyunki chhota insaan hi sabse zyada seekh sakta hai.

— aur main seekhta raha —

Chapter 03

Tootna

jab sab kuch bikhar gaya

Har kahani mein ek aisa mod aata hai, jahan sab kuch tootne lagta hai. Mere saath bhi aaya.

Kuch khaas nahi hua tha — bas zindagi ne apna kaam kiya. Log chale gaye, sapne toote, bharosa hil gaya. Aur main akela reh gaya — apne hi andheron ke saath.

Wahi sabse mushkil waqt tha.

Raat bhar jaagta tha. Kuch sochta nahi tha, bas jaagta tha. Subah hote hi lagta tha — aaj phir se wahi din. Koi rasta nahi dikhta tha. Koi manzil nahi.

"Tum theek ho?"
— kisi ne poocha.
"Haan," maine kaha.
Jhooth bola tha.

Par ek din — pata nahi kaise — main utha. Nahaya. Kapde pehne. Bahar nikla. Aur chai pee. Bas itna hi. Par us din kuch badla.

· · ·

Tootna zaroori hai. Kyunki jab sab kuch toot jaata hai, tab pata chalta hai — asli cheez kya thi. Jo bacha, wahi sach tha.

Chapter 04

Uthna

phir se khade hone ki kahani

Uthna aasan nahi tha. Par ek baar uth gaya, toh phir rukna nahi tha.

Chhoti chhoti cheezein — ek acchi chai, ek purana gaana, ek dost ka message — inhi ne sahara diya. Bade sapne nahi, chhote chhote lamhe.

Aur wahi lamhe zindagi ban gaye.

Maine likhna shuru kiya. Pehle bas apne liye. Phir kisi ne padha, aur kaha — "yeh toh meri bhi kahani hai". Tab samajh aaya, kahaniyan sirf apni nahi hoti. Sabki hoti hain.

"Tum likhte ho?"
— kisi ne poocha.
"Haan," maine kaha. "Ab toh likhta hoon."

"Jo girta hai, wahi
uthna seekhta hai."

Chapter 05

Aaj

jahaan main ab hoon

Aaj main yahan hoon. Wahi bachcha, par thoda samajhdaar. Wahi sapne, par thoda sach ke qareeb.

Ab bhi galtiyan karta hoon. Ab bhi seekh raha hoon. Farq sirf itna hai — ab apne aap pe sharm nahi aati.

Yeh kahani abhi khatam nahi hui.

Bas ek page palta hai. Baaki tum padhte raho — main likhta rahunga.

— shukriya, padhne ke liye —
